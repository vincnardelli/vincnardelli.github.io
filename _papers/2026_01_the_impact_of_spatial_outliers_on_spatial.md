# Abstract
This research addresses how abnormal observations distort spatial analysis. The authors introduce the Local Influence Function (LIF), a modified approach that incorporates spatial dependence into robustness analysis. Unlike traditional influence function methods, the LIF accounts for how an observation's effect depends not only on its value but also on its geographic location, neighboring connections, and neighboring values. Through simulated and empirical data, the study demonstrates that LIF offers improved detection of spatial outlier effects in correlation analysis compared to the local Moran index.
